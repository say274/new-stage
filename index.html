Dearest Linda
For every child with whom
you shared the gift of Jesus
For every weary heart
you made room for
For all the things you did
when nobody was watching
Thank you
As you begin this new journey
May God meet you there with
wisdom, courage and joy
And give you even more reasons to dance
It is my prayer that those you meet will see Jesus
a little more clearly
Just as I was able to through you

See, I am doing a new thing.

Now it springs up;

do you not perceive it?

Isaiah 43:19
